All about Isaac Newton

Isaac Newton

Sir Isaac Newton (1642–1727) was an English polymath and culminating figure of the Scientific Revolution. He revolutionized physics and mathematics by formulating the laws of motion and universal gravitation, discovering the composition of white light, and independently developing infinitesimal calculus.
